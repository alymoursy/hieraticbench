# Wikimedia Commons (wm-*)

Collected 2026-10-05 via the Commons MediaWiki API (categories Hieratic, Hieratic inscriptions/ostraka, Museo Egizio Turin hieratic/demotic subcategories, document categories, and `list=search`). Every image was viewed; crops remove captions, labels and scale bars. JPEG q90, long edge at most 2000 px, EXIF stripped. Originals were not stored in the repo.

61 items: 47 hieratic, 1 abnormal-hieratic, 7 demotic (control), 6 cursive-hieroglyphic (control). All `split: public`, `rungs: [identify]`.

## Notable rejections

- **Brooklyn Museum Flickr uploads** (Saite Oracle Papyrus 47.218.3, which is abnormal hieratic; Brooklyn snake papyrus 47.218.48; Book of the Dead sheets). Their license is 'No known copyright restrictions' ('No restrictions'), which is not on the accepted list, so they were left out.
- **Louvre E 3228 B** (abnormal hieratic loan deed, CC BY-SA). Image too blurry at 1280 px.
- **Berlin Book of the Breathing photo** (Osama Shukir). The description says hieratic but the image shows cursive hieroglyphs. Dropped.
- **Book of the Dead of Neferini**: one Commons file calls it hieratic and another calls it cursive hieroglyphs. Dropped.
- **MET Book of the Dead of Imhotep** and **MET Book of the Dead ch. 100/129**: the script looks hieratic or mixed and the source says nothing. Dropped.
- **Faint or illegible pieces**: Turin ostraca S 6378, S 9619, S 9611, S 9596, S 9618, S 9588; Birmingham limestone board; KV54 linen; Gebelein fragments (script not stated); Dispute of a Man with his Ba (blurry); Naples demotic papyrus (tiny text); Turin Kemyt hieroglyphic ostracon (too few signs to be sure).
- **Duplicates or overrepresented documents**: Cleveland photo of Harris I (same sheet as wm-0018), Kagemni facsimile and BnF Prisse 192 (Prisse already has 2 items), Papyrus Berlin 10499 and Lepsius Hieratischer Papyrus I (more Sinuhe), and the Captmondo photo of EA 10800 (same object as wm-0048).
- **Low resolution or undated**: Rhind 750 px photo, Moscow problem 14 scan, Papyrus Sallier II (598 px), Papyrus Berlin 15338 (no date), LACMA 'Ramesside' ostracon (script uncertain at that size).
